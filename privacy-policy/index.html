Privacy Policy — VocabSprout

Last updated: February 15, 2026

VocabSprout (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) respects your privacy.

Information We Collect

VocabSprout does not require account registration and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

The app does not collect, transmit, or store user-identifiable data.

In-App Purchases

If you choose to start a free trial, subscribe, or purchase lifetime access, payments are processed securely by Apple through the App Store.

We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple may collect and process data as described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Data Stored on Your Device

Any app data (such as saved words, preferences, or progress) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Third-Party Services

VocabSprout does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services.

Children’s Privacy

VocabSprout does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the “Last updated” date above.
