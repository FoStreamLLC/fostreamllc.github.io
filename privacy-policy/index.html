Privacy Policy — VocabSprout

Last updated: August 9, 2026

VocabSprout (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) respects your privacy. VocabSprout is designed to provide vocabulary study tools without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information.

Information We Collect

VocabSprout does not require user registration or login.

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, or other personally identifiable information.

The app does not require you to provide personal information in order to use its features.

Data Stored on Your Device

App data, such as saved words, study progress, completed decks, and preferences, is stored locally on your device.

This information is used only to provide the app’s study and personalization features and is not transmitted to us.

Advertising

VocabSprout does not display advertisements.

There are no banner ads, rewarded ads, video ads, or other third-party advertising within the app.

Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

VocabSprout does not offer subscriptions or in-app purchases.

All study features are available to users free of charge.

Account Registration

VocabSprout does not require an account, registration, or login. Users can access the app’s features without providing personal information.

Children’s Privacy

VocabSprout does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the app does not require account registration or the submission of personal information, users can access its educational features without providing personal data to us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the app or our privacy practices.

If we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this policy.
