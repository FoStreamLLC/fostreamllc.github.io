Privacy Policy — NoisyButtons

Last updated: September 16, 2026

NoisyButtons ("we," "our," or "the app") is developed by FoStream LLC. We respect your privacy and are committed to providing a simple and safe app experience.

NoisyButtons is designed for children and families and does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to use the app.

Information We Collect

NoisyButtons does not require account registration, login, or the submission of personal information.

We do not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location
  • Photos or videos
  • Contacts
  • Personal identifiers

Data Stored on Your Device

NoisyButtons may store certain app settings and preferences locally on your device, such as sound preferences, haptic preferences, button theme selections, and other app settings.

This information is used only to provide and remember your preferred app experience. It is stored locally on the device and is not transmitted to FoStream LLC.

Advertising

NoisyButtons does not display advertisements.

There are no banner ads, rewarded ads, video ads, or third-party advertising networks within the app.

Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

NoisyButtons does not offer subscriptions or in-app purchases.

The app is available to use without requiring additional purchases.

Analytics and Tracking

NoisyButtons does not use third-party advertising tracking and does not track users across apps or websites for advertising purposes.

Account Registration

NoisyButtons does not require an account, registration, or login.

Users can access the app's features without providing personal information.

Children's Privacy

NoisyButtons is designed with young children and families in mind.

We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through the app.

The app does not provide social networking, messaging, chat, or user-generated content features.

External Links

NoisyButtons may contain a link intended for parents or older users to learn about another FoStream LLC app. External links intended for adults are protected by a parental gate where appropriate.

When a user leaves NoisyButtons and accesses an external website or the App Store, the privacy practices of that external service apply.

Data Sharing

FoStream LLC does not sell personal information collected through NoisyButtons.

Because NoisyButtons does not intentionally collect personal information through its normal app experience, we do not share such information with advertisers or data brokers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to NoisyButtons, applicable requirements, or our privacy practices.

If changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.
